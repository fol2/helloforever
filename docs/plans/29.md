   - `tools/strip1.py` (new): the format and nothing else, no I/O. Pack and unpack the fast
     block, the frame and an object; bytes to squares at both depths; the row as a list of
     colours (the reference encoder); read a row from a bitmap; the reader's rules for
     joining chunks and for asking for the fall-back.
   - `tests/strip1_test.py` (new): every field through an image at both depths, the
     refusals, the joining rules, the vectors of the spec.
   - `docs/plans/29.md` (this file), `.github/workflows/ci.yml`, `docs/code-standards.md`.
2. **The row in the addon**
   - `HelloLink/Strip.lua` (new) and `HelloLink/HelloLink.toc`: the always-on row, the level
     squares, the carousel and its objects, the settings, the look at a slot for the
     fall-back. `HelloLink/HelloLink.lua` keeps the probes until the row is seen in the game.
   - `tests/strip1_addon_test.lua` (new): the addon in the stub client draws a row, the
     Python reader of step 1 reads it; every field, both depths, the fall-back.
   - A new file needs the client to restart: the install waits for the game to close.
3. **The reader that follows the row**
   - `tools/hellolink_strip.py`: `watch [--json]`, the request for the fall-back through
     `tools/hellolink.py` (the `strip` field of a slot).
   - `tools/strip_capture.swift` (new): the capture stream, pixels to a pipe, no knowledge

4. **Acceptance in the game, then the standing reference**

   - `docs/STRIP.md` (new) with the test vectors, `AGENTS.md` routes to it; the spec keeps

## Risks

- The Lua encoder and the Python reader disagree: step 2's test reads the one with the
  other, field by field, at both depths.
- The client's Deflate is not what Python reads (spec, section 11): the object says whether
  it is compressed, the addon falls back to uncompressed when it has no compressor, and the
  reader tries a raw stream and then a zlib one. Seen in the game in step 4.
- Six bits a square do not hold: the fall-back; the session squares are two-level.
- The row costs frame time: step 2 measures a rebuild in its test's stub and step 4 in play;
  only changed squares are recoloured.
- The Swift part cannot run in CI: it holds no format logic, and step 3 tests the pipe's
  framing in Python.
