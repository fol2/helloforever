# Spec: #115 The assistant

A bar in the game that shows the player one piece of advice at a time, each a **card**: a
line of text that says what it stands on. Cards come from rules in the addon, from a
program on the player's computer (the **companion**), and later from AI. It is priority 3
of `docs/north-star.md`: what the game saved comes back to the player as advice, in the
game. The owner's direction, as the north star quotes it: a personal assistant for his own
play first, "全方位嘅私人助理", with its own UI and not a chat bot, which may connect to AI.

Status: draft for build. Nothing of it is built. A line marked **Seen** was seen in the
game and names where that is recorded. A line marked **To probe** is an assumption; section
13 lists what settles it.

## 1. What exists and what this adds

| Exists | Where |
|---|---|
| A way out of the game, live: the strip and its reader, with a local API | `docs/STRIP.md` |
| A way in, without a reload: the slot addons, one used by every look (`plan/05-assistant-link.md`, section 1); 200 a UI session as installed | `docs/STRIP.md`, section 7 |
| The saved data: every fight, the snapshot and the fight baseline; the level log, which the schema does not describe yet | `docs/SCHEMA.md`; `HelloForever/Snapshot.lua` |
| A panel and a fight window; one chat line after a fight (`/hello baseline on`) | `HelloForever/UI.lua`, `docs/specs/93.md` |

This spec adds: the bar and the card (sections 3 and 4), the first cards of the addon
(section 5), the way in for cards from outside and its acknowledgement on the strip
(section 6), the companion (section 7) and the AI step (section 8).

## 2. Decisions

The lead's, under the owner's "你係 project lead，你可以決定" (12 Nov 2026), unless a line
quotes the owner.

1. **A bar with cards, not a chat.** Owner: "我唔係諗住喺入面開個 chat bot". The bar shows
   one card. There is no conversation: a question is one of a few fixed words, and it gets
   one answer, as a card.
2. **A rule lives in the addon wherever the addon holds the data.** Then it works for
   anyone who installs HelloForever, with no program outside and no private data
   (`docs/specs/93.md`, decision 1). The companion is for what the addon cannot hold:
   history older than the addon keeps, knowledge about the game, and AI.
3. **One card format for every adviser.** The bar does not care who made a card. The card
   says who.
4. **Every card says what it stands on.** "8 fights at level 14", "bags now". The
   assistant's value is that it knows this character's history (`docs/north-star.md`,
   non-goals); a player can check a card only when it names its ground. A card without one
   is not shown.
5. **Unknown gives no card.** A rule whose input is missing, secret or not as expected says
   nothing. It never guesses (`docs/specs/29.md`, decision 6).
6. **Advice, not automation.** A card is text. A click on the bar opens a card's detail or
   sends it away; nothing on the bar casts, uses, sells or moves. The companion reads the
   reader's API and writes slot files, and does nothing else to the game.
7. **Quiet in a fight.** Nothing on the bar changes in combat. A card made in a fight waits
   for its end. No look at a slot is made in combat.
8. **The assistant cannot break the capture** (priority 1). It is a file of its own in
   HelloForever; every entry into it runs under `pcall`; a rule that fails three times is
   off for the session and `/hello debug` says so. A rule after a fight runs after the
   record is saved. With the bar off the addon behaves as 0.11.0 does.
9. **A message from outside is whole.** The slot file always holds every current card from
   outside. The addon replaces its set with the newest one. A look that was not made loses
   nothing (`docs/specs/29.md`, decision 2).
10. **Looks are few.** A UI session has 200 slots as installed, and a look uses one. The
    addon looks at fixed times, at a few moments and after a question (section 6). The
    probe of section 13 may show a better way.
11. **AI is one more adviser, behind the companion, and waits for an Expenditure
    decision** (`docs/owner-decisions.md`). Steps 1 to 3 spend nothing.
12. **Off by default**, as `announce` and `baseline` are: `/hello assist on`.
13. **A card earns its place by a gap: what other addons cannot do.** Owner, 15 Nov 2026,
    on which parts of play come first: "1. Fight, 2. Gear, 3. Gold, 4. Supplies, 5. Quest;
    I rank this by gap that other addons cannot do". What other addons cannot do is know
    this character's fights. So a card that stands on the saved fights comes before one
    that any addon could show, and the parts are built in his order (section 5).
14. **The player chooses the language.** Owner, 15 Nov 2026: "Can choose between
    languages, default English, an option to support Cantonese. (Other options support
    other major languages)". Every text of the assistant comes from a table, one a
    language; English and Cantonese are the first two (section 4).
15. **Nothing needs typing.** Owner, 15 Nov 2026: "The gui is needed" and "The configs are
    not only accessible by cmd". Every setting and every action of the assistant has a
    control in its window (section 4). The slash commands stay as a second way to the same
    settings.

## 3. The card

| Field | Type | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `id` | a word of letters, digits and hyphens, at most 24 | The subject. |
| `kind` | `state` or `event` | `state`: something that holds now (two bag slots free). `event`: something that happened (a fight ended). |
| `prio` | 1, 2 or 3 | 1: deal with it before you go on. 2: worth a look. 3: good to know. |
| `text` | UTF-8, one line, at most 120 bytes | What the bar shows. |
| `basis` | UTF-8, one line, at most 120 bytes | What the card stands on. Needed. |
| `detail` | UTF-8, at most 6 lines and 600 bytes, or none | More, in the tooltip. |
| `src` | `addon`, `companion` or `ai` | Who made it. |
| `at` | whole seconds, the time the card was made | The start of `ttl`, and the age the tooltip shows. |
| `ttl` | whole seconds, 10 to 3600 | An `event` card goes this long after `at`. A `state` card ignores it: it stays while its rule gives it, or while the sets from outside carry it. |

- **The key.** A card of the addon is known by its `id`; a card from outside by `out:` and
  its `id`. A newer card with the same key takes the older one's place. So a card from
  outside never takes the place of one of the addon's, and muting and the counts of
  section 9 go by the key.
- **Order.** The bar shows the card with the lowest `prio`; among equals, the newest. The
  others wait behind it.
- **Sent away.** A right-click sends the shown card away. An `event` card is gone; one
  from outside does not come back with a later set that still carries it (the same key and
  `at`). A `state` card stays away until its condition has ended once (the rule gives it
  no more, or a set from outside comes without it): it shows again when the condition
  comes back. It also shows again at once when its `prio` number goes down (two bag slots
  free became none). A change of the numbers in its text
  alone does not bring it back.
- **Muted.** `/hello assist mute <key>` keeps a subject away for good; it is saved.
- At most 20 cards are held. When a twenty-first comes, the oldest `event` card of the
  highest `prio` number goes; with no `event` card, the new card is refused.

## 4. The bar

A frame of its own, `HelloForeverAssistBar`, one line high, under the XP panel and as
wide. It takes the panel's scale and lock. It is shown only while there is a card, also when the
panel is hidden. Step 1 does not let the player move it apart from the panel.

    ▌Arrows: 140 left                                              +2

- The edge's colour is the `prio`: red for 1, yellow for 2, grey for 3. `+2` is how many
  cards wait. A card of `src` `ai` has the letters `AI` before its text.
- Hover: the tooltip has the `detail`, then one line: who made it, the `basis`, how long
  ago. `from the addon · 8 fights at level 14 · 2 min ago`.
- Left-click: the assistant's window (below).
- Right-click: sends the shown card away (section 3).
- In combat the bar keeps what it shows; clicks do nothing (decision 7).

**The assistant's window** (decision 15). One window, `HelloForeverAssistWindow`, built
from plain frames, buttons and check buttons as the fight window is. It opens from a
left-click on the bar, from the entry `Assistant` in the panel's right-click menu, from a
button in the fight window's head, and by `/hello assist`. So it opens with the bar off,
with no card, and with the panel hidden. It has two parts:

- **Cards.** Every card, in the bar's order, each with its basis, who made it and its
  age; on each row a button to send it away and one to mute its subject.
- **Settings.** Each is saved at once:

| Control | Setting | The command for the same |
|---|---|---|
| A check button, `Show the bar` | `settings.assist` | `/hello assist on\|off` |
| A row of buttons, one a language | `settings.assistLang` | `/hello assist lang <code>` |
| A check button, `Take cards from the companion` | `settings.assistOutside` | `/hello assist outside on\|off` |
| The muted subjects, each with a button to take it back | `settings.assistMuted` | `/hello assist unmute <key>` |
| A button, `Show a sample card` | none | `/hello assist test` |
| From step 2: a button for each topic to ask | none | `/hello ask <topic>` |
| From step 2: one line of state: when a set was last taken, how many slots are left, or that the strip is off | none | none |

The window's own words come from the language table. In combat the window can be open,
and its controls do nothing until the fight ends (decision 7). The panel's right-click
menu also gets a check box for the bar; that menu is built with the client's `MenuUtil`,
which is **not seen** in the game yet (`HelloForever/HISTORY.md`, the unverified list), so
no setting is reachable through the menu alone. **To probe**: whether this client lets an
addon add a page to the game's own settings; if it does, the page has one button that
opens this window.

Commands, the second way to the same things:

| Command | Effect |
|---|---|
| `/hello assist on\|off` | The bar on or off (off by default) |
| `/hello assist` | Opens the assistant's window |
| `/hello assist list` | The current cards in chat, each with its basis |
| `/hello assist mute <key>` / `unmute <key>` | Keep a subject away for good, or take it back |
| `/hello assist test` | A sample card for 30 s, in the chosen language: to see the bar and where it sits |
| `/hello assist outside on\|off` | Take cards from the companion or not (on by default; it matters only with HelloLink and a companion) |
| `/hello ask <topic>` | Ask the companion (section 6). The window has a button for each topic. |
| `/hello assist lang <code>` | The language of the cards: `en` (the default) or `yue`, Cantonese; with no code, the languages there are |

**Languages** (decision 14). A language is one table of texts, named by its code: `en`,
and `yue` for Cantonese in traditional characters. A card's text is a pattern from the
table with the numbers and names put in; a name (an enemy, an item) is as the client gives
it. A pattern that a table lacks is taken from `en`. Another language is one more table,
in a pull request of its own, read by someone who speaks it; a test holds that every table
has every pattern of `en` with the same places for numbers. The limits of section 3 are
bytes, and a Chinese character is three: a line of 120 bytes holds 40. **Seen**: Chinese
text from a slot shows correctly in chat (12 Nov 2026, `plan/05`, section 1); on the bar's
font it is **to probe**, in step 1's game check. The command's own words and `/hello`'s
help stay English.

The chat line of `/hello baseline on` stays as it is. The `fight` card and that line are
made from the same numbers by one function.

Each rule runs on a client event or after a saved fight, out of combat, and reads through
HelloForever's guarded calls (`Core.lua`). None computes on a secret value.

first part. Each later card is a pull request of its own, taken in this order; a card that
waits for a probe does not hold back the one after it.

**Not ranked** by the owner, so after the five parts and only with a gap of their own:
`level` (how long this level took against the one before, from the level log), `talent`
(points to spend), `sell` (what the grey items are worth to a vendor), `repair` (gear about
to break), `enchant` (a weapon's temporary enchant about to end; **Seen** readable,
`plan/05`, P2).

**How a card is added.** One small pull request a card. It says what the card stands on and
where that was seen in the game, or it ships the probe first (`LESSONS.md`, "Probe a client
capability in the game before building a feature on it"). It has a harness test for the
card, one for its absence when the input is unknown or secret, and a game check in `plan/`.

## 6. The way in: cards from outside

**The slot file** gains a field and a third part. Each part has one writer:

```lua
HelloLink_Inbox = { seq = 1794516378971, text = "", slots = 200,
  strip = { session = 4711, depth = 2 },
  assist = { session = 4711, seq = 12, t = 1794516378, re = 3, cards = {
    { id = "session", kind = "event", prio = 3, src = "companion",
      at = 1794516370, ttl = 600,
      text = "This session: 42 fights, 1 death, 14,200 XP an hour",
      basis = "the strip since 20:14" },
  } } }
```

- `slots` is how many slot addons there are. The tool counts them and writes the number at
  every write. The addon does not count them itself: it learns the number at its first look,
  and from then on knows how many are left.
- `assist.session` is the UI session the set is for: the session number of the strip
  (`docs/specs/29.md`, section 4), which the companion reads from the strip.
- `assist.seq` changes when the cards or `re` change. The addon orders nothing by it: the
  set of its latest look is the current one. Object 13 gives it back, so that the
  companion sees which set the game holds.
- `assist.t` is the time of the write, in seconds, and changes at every write.
- `assist.re` is the number of the last question of that session that was answered, or
  absent when none was. Every later set of the session carries it on, also a set written
  by a companion that was started again, which takes it from object 13.
- A write replaces its own part and carries the others over, under the lock
  `tools/hellolink.py` already takes. A write of `strip` or `assist` keeps the top `seq`
  and `text`, so `/hlink` does not show an old text as new.
- The companion writes `assist` at least once a minute.

**What HelloLink takes**, at a look. It checks the whole part once, strictly (`LESSONS.md`,
"A gate that reads a config file validates the whole file once, strictly"):

- `assist` is a table; `session`, `seq`, `t` and `re` are whole numbers in range; `cards`
  is a list of at most 8.
- The set is taken only when `assist.session` is the addon's own session of now, as the
  strip's own request is (`docs/specs/29.md`, section 3). A set left from before a
  `/reload` does nothing, and an old answer cannot meet a new question.
- The set is taken only when `t` is no more than 180 s before the client's `time()` and
  no more than 5 s after it: its companion was alive a moment ago. **To probe**: that the
  client's `time()` is the computer's clock.
- A set that is taken replaces every card from outside. A set that is not taken removes
  them.
- A card is taken when every field is as section 3 says, `src` is `companion` or `ai`,
  and `at` is not after `t`. A card that is not is dropped and counted; the others are
  taken. A field of a card that the addon does not know is ignored.
- Before a text from outside is shown, every `|` in it is doubled, so that no colour,
  texture or link code from outside is drawn. **To probe**: that a doubled `|` shows as
  one and starts no code.

A card's text is data and never code: the companion writes it only through the tool's Lua
string encoder, and a test feeds that encoder quotes, backslashes, `]]`, line breaks and
bytes that are not UTF-8.

**How long a set holds.** The addon learns about the companion only at a look. The cards
of a set that was taken stay until the next look: an `event` card goes by its `ttl`
before that, a `state` card does not. So a companion that stopped is noticed at the next
look, at most 10 minutes later, and one that started is noticed then, or at once by a
question. That is the limit of looking at fixed times; the doorbell of section 13 would
lift it.

**The way in needs the strip.** The session number and the acknowledgement are the
strip's. With `/hlink strip off` HelloLink makes no look for the assistant and there is no
object 13; the cards from outside go, the bar shows the addon's cards only, and `/hello
ask` says in chat that the strip is off and asks nothing.

**The hand-over.** HelloLink and HelloForever are two addons and call no code of each
other (`docs/specs/93.md`, step 3):

- HelloLink leaves what it took in the plain global `HelloLinkAssist`: `{strip, session,
  seq, t, re, cards, left, taken, refused}`; `strip` is false while the strip is off.
  HelloForever reads it with `rawget` once a second, out of combat, and checks it again.
- HelloForever leaves the plain global `HelloForeverAssist`: `{on, lang, ask = {n, topic,
  t}, shown}`. HelloLink reads it with `rawget` once a second.
- A table that is missing or not as described counts as absent. Each addon runs as before
  without the other.

**When HelloLink looks.** A look loads the next slot. It serves the strip's fall-back and
the assistant at once.

- At 30 s, 1, 2 and 5 minutes after the UI loaded and every 10 minutes after that: the
  strip's times of today. Today they stop when the strip is not in `auto` at depth 4; with
  `HelloForeverAssist.on` they go on at any depth.
- At a moment: the character starts resting; a level up.
- After a question, whether a companion was seen before or not: 3 s, 8 s and 20 s after
  it, until a set of this session comes whose `re` is the question's number. Without one
  the `ask` card says `no answer: is the companion running?`.
- Never in combat; a look that falls due in a fight is made after it.
- When 20 slots are left, only a question makes a look. When none is left, a question
  gives the card `no slot left: /reload gives them back`.

In a session of four hours the fixed times use 27 slots. Each moment uses one, and each
question up to 3.

**Asking.** A topic's button in the assistant's window, or `/hello ask <topic>`, while
HelloLink is loaded and the strip is on. A topic is a word of letters and hyphens, at most
24. The bar shows the `event` card `ask` ("asked: session") until the answer comes or 30 s
pass. The question's number `n` goes up by one from 1 in a UI session; the set's `session`
ties an answer to it. The question carries the time it was asked, `t`. Step 3 has one
topic, `session`; the AI step adds more. Free text stays `/hlink send` (the strip's object
1); only the AI step answers it.

**The acknowledgement: strip object 13, `assist`.** `docs/specs/29.md`, object 1: "How an
answer comes back is the assistant's spec". The companion learns from the strip what the
game has taken:

    {"on": true, "lang": "en", "seq": 12, "re": 3,
     "ask": {"n": 4, "topic": "session", "t": 1794516375},
     "left": 163, "taken": 1, "refused": 0, "shown": "bags"}

- `seq` and `re`: of the last set taken in this session, `null` before the first. `ask`:
  the last question; `null` before the first. `left`: the session's slots not yet used,
  `null` before the first look. `taken`, `refused`: the cards of the last set. `shown`:
  the key of the card on the bar, or `null`.
- `lang`: the language the player chose (section 4). The companion writes its cards in
  it, and in `en` when it has no table for it.
- The session is the frame's own (`docs/specs/29.md`, section 4).
- `null` for the whole object when HelloForever's table is absent or the bar is off.
- Looked at every second; a new version when it differs.
- With thirteen objects an object is stale after 46.8 s at the default `dwell` of 0.1 s
  (`3 x 13 x 12 x dwell`; it is 43.2 s with twelve). A reader's object count changes with
  it. Agreed with the local program before it is built, as every format change.

## 7. The companion

`tools/assistant.py`: standard-library Python, tested in CI, run beside the reference
reader on the player's computer.

    python3 tools/hellolink_strip.py watch --serve 8787 --addons <AddOns>
    python3 tools/assistant.py --addons <AddOns> [--reader http://127.0.0.1:8787]

- It listens to the reader's `/events` (`docs/STRIP.md`, section 10): the snapshot first,
  then every event. It starts no capture of its own: one display takes one stream.
- It makes cards only from a state whose `condition` is `live`. While the reader says
  `stale`, `lost` or `waiting` its sets carry no `state` card and no new `event` card; an
  `event` card it gave before stays in the sets until its `ttl` has passed.
- It answers a question when object 13 shows an `ask` whose `n` is not the `re` beside
  it and whose `t` is no more than 60 s ago: it writes a set for that session with that
  `re`, and every later set of the session carries that `re` on (section 6). A question
  older than 60 s is not answered: the bar has said `no answer` by then. A companion that
  was started again reads object 13 before its first write and takes the `re` from it, so
  it answers no question twice and needs no memory of its own.
- **Step 3's one topic, `session`**: since the companion began to follow this UI session,
  how many counted fights and deaths (object 12), the XP an hour and the money gained (the
  fast block), and the enemy that took longest against its usual time. It stands on the
  strip alone: `basis` is `the strip since <time>`.
- In step 3 it keeps no state on disk and no screenshot; the AI step adds one file, for
  its limits (section 8). `--log <file>` writes the sets it sent.
- It reads only the reader's API. It writes only slot files. It presses no key and
  touches no window.
- It runs where the reader runs: macOS today. Once the reader is installed outside any
  git checkout (#117, open), the companion is installed and started the same way; the two
  commands above are then those of the installed folder.

Not in step 3: reading the saved data file. The fight log in it is compressed by the
client, and this repository has no reader for it (the offline pipeline's is private).
History from the saved file is a later step with its own intent.

## 8. AI (step 4, after an Expenditure decision)

On a question, the companion sends a **brief** to a model and turns the answer into cards
of `src` `ai`.

- **The brief** is a list of numbered facts from the strip: class and level, the zone,
  the guide's step and next steps, the bags, the quests, the last fights against their
  usual values, the session so far, and the question. An id without a name (an item, a
  creature) is not sent as a bare number; the step's plan says where names come from.
- **The answer** is at most 3 cards, each naming the numbers of the facts it stands on.
  The companion writes the `basis` from those facts; the model does not. A card that names
  no fact, or a fact that is not in the brief, is dropped.
- **The language.** The answer is asked for in the player's language (object 13's
  `lang`), whatever language the question was typed in.
- **When it calls.** Only on the player's question. Never on a timer.
- **Limits.** A number of calls a day and an amount of money a month, both kept by the
  companion in a local file, the one thing it keeps on disk, so that a restart does not
  forget them. At a limit the answer is a card that says so.
- **What leaves the computer.** The brief and nothing else: no character name, realm or
  account, no screenshot. `--show-brief` prints the brief and sends nothing.
- **The key** comes from the environment and is never in the repository, a slot file or a
  log.
- **What the model knows about the game may be about another version of it.** The prompt
  tells it to advise from the facts only and never to advise automation. The bar marks its
  cards `AI`.
- **Before it is turned on**: a set of briefs with made-up characters, kept in the
  repository, is run against the model at every change of the prompt or the model. Each
  answer is checked for its shape and its facts, and read once for advice that the facts
  do not carry.

The provider, the model, the price of a question measured on a real brief, and the monthly

## 9. Storage (`docs/SCHEMA.md` gains a section in step 1)

```
settings.assist = false                 -- the bar
settings.assistOutside = true           -- take cards from outside
settings.assistLang = "en"              -- the language of the cards
settings.assistMuted = { bags = true, ["out:session"] = true }  -- keys kept away for good
characters[key].assist = { ["<card key>"] = { shown = <count>, away = <count>, t = <last time> } }
```

- Cards are not saved. After a reload the rules give them again.
- `characters[key].assist` counts, for each card key (section 3), how often a card was
  shown and how often it was sent away: at most 50 keys, the one with the oldest `t` going first. It
  is the evidence for "is it good" (the owner shares the assistant "如果佢真係好好").
- Fight records do not change.

## 10. Limits and non-goals

- No control of the game, by the addon or by the companion (`docs/owner-decisions.md`,
  2026-11-12).
- No advice in a fight on what to press: that is HelloAim's subject and the trial of Matty
  Hunter alone (`HelloAim/PLAN.md`).
- No conversation: a question has no memory of the one before it.
- No AI call without an approved expenditure.
- Not in this spec: sound; advice for a group; data about other players; a reader or a
  companion for Windows; cards for other programs to read, beyond `shown` in object 13.
